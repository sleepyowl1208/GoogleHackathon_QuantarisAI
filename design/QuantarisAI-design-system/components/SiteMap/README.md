# SiteMap
Location Intelligence: candidate sites, raw materials, clusters and ports placed by coordinates over a neutral dot grid — India is felt through the spread of real places, never drawn as an outline.

**Provide:** `sites` (`{id, name, lat, lon, kind: candidate | cluster | raw | port | risk, selected?, note?, label?}`), `links` (`{from, to, selected?}` logistics/resource relationships), `illustrative` (default true), `label`, `width`/`height`.

- No state or national borders, flags or fills. The geography is the data.
- The selected candidate is the one saffron square; its supply links are `saffron-strong`. Everything else is blue/ink.
- Demo or modelled data MUST keep the "≈ Illustrative data · not live" caption. Never imply live government data.
- Shapes carry kind (square, circle, diamond, triangle) so colour is never the only cue; the legend says so.
